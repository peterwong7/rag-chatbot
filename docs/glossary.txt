Glossary
=========

tf-idf

tf-idf stands for term frequency-inverse
document frequency. It scores a word by
how often it appears in one document,
compared with how often it appears across
all documents. A word that appears a lot in
one document but rarely anywhere else gets
a high score. A word that appears almost
everywhere, like "the", gets a low score.
Lecture 6 uses this idea to turn text into
numbers the AI can work with, and this
chatbot's own retriever uses it too.

Cross-entropy

Cross-entropy is the loss function a neural
network uses when it classifies things. It
measures how far the network's predicted
probabilities are from the right answer. A
perfect prediction gives a loss near zero,
and a confident wrong prediction gives a
large loss. Lecture 5 uses
binary_crossentropy for two-way choices and
categorical_crossentropy for many-way
choices.

Word embedding

A word embedding is a distributed
representation of a word as a short vector
of numbers. Words with similar meanings end
up with similar vectors, so the AI can tell
that "book" is close to "essay" but far from
"car". Lecture 6 builds embeddings with
word2vec, a neural network that predicts a
word from the words around it.
